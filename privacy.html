Privacy

Last updated 29 October 2026

In short

  • Ikna works fully without an account. Then your cards stay only on your device.
  • If you turn on sync, your cards are stored in the EU so they can be the same on all your devices.
  • No ads, no tracking, no analytics. Your data is never sold.
  • You can download or delete everything at any time.

Without an account

Your decks, cards, progress, pictures and settings are kept in your browser (or in the Android app) on your device. Nothing is sent to us. Backups you make are files you keep yourself.

With an account (sync)

What's stored: your email address (to sign you in), your decks, cards, study history and progress, settings, and the pictures and sounds in your cards. If you sign in with Google, we only receive your email address from Google.

Why: to give you the sync you asked for (the legal basis is the contract with you). We use it for nothing else.

Where: with Supabase, our hosting provider, in a data centre in the European Union. Supabase processes the data for us under a data processing agreement. Your data is sent encrypted (HTTPS), and each account can only read its own data.

How long: until you delete your account. Deleted cards are remembered as “deleted” for up to 90 days so your other devices delete them too.

Your rights: in Settings → Account and sync you can Download my data (a copy of everything) and Delete my account (this removes your account and everything stored online; cards on your device stay until you delete them there). You can also ask us to correct or delete your data, and you can complain to your data protection authority.

Backups: keep your own backup (Settings → Back up) as a safety net; we can't restore deleted data for you.

AI card maker (optional)

The AI features use your own API key from OpenAI or Anthropic. Your key is stored only on your device. It is never put in backups, exports or sync, and it never leaves your device except to go to the AI company you chose with each request.

Only when you press an ✨ AI button, the notes, photo or card you're working on are sent straight from your device to that company. They don't pass through Ikna. That company's own privacy policy applies to what it receives (OpenAI, Anthropic).

Children

If you are under 16, please ask a parent or guardian before you create an account. (The age differs by country in the EU, from 13 to 16.) You can use Ikna without an account at any age.

Contact
