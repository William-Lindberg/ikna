<h1 align="center">Ikna</h1>

<p align="center"><b>Remember anything.</b> Flashcards with smart spaced repetition, for exams, facts,
definitions, formulas, names or new words. As capable as Anki, but much easier to understand.</p>

## Install

**Web:** open <https://william-lindberg.github.io/ikna/>. It works offline after the first visit.
In Chrome or Edge choose *Install app*; on a phone use *Add to Home screen*.

**Android:** on your phone, open [the latest release](https://github.com/William-Lindberg/ikna/releases/latest),
download `Ikna-x.y.z.apk` and open it. The first time, Android asks you to allow installing apps
from your browser or file manager.

## What it does

- Add cards in seconds: type `question = answer`, paste a list, tap words to hide them, or snap a photo.
- Basic, both-ways, type-the-answer, fill-in-the-blank and hide-parts-of-an-image cards, with formulas, images and sound.
- Smart scheduling (FSRS), an exam planner, exam simulation and comeback mode after a break.
- Import Anki decks (.apkg) with your progress, Quizlet and spreadsheets; share decks; print or save as PDF.
- Optional free account to sync between phone and computer (email or Google).
- Optional AI card maker with your own OpenAI or Claude key.

## Is it safe?

- **No ads, no tracking.** Without an account, your cards never leave your device.
- **Few permissions.** The Android app only asks for the camera or photos when you add a picture to a
  card, and for notifications if you turn on daily reminders.
- **Signed by the author.** Every APK is signed with the same key. Its SHA-256 certificate fingerprint is

- **Privacy:** see the [privacy page](https://william-lindberg.github.io/ikna/privacy.html).
